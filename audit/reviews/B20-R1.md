## Required changes
1. [scope] `core/politeness.py:PolitenessEngine.pace_wire_send`, `core/http_client.py:_WatchdogHTTPAdapter` (the `conn.request` wrapper, `pool_connections/pool_maxsize=64`, and the new `politeness` argument to `_RequestWatchdog`), and `test_b19_scanner_robustness.py:180-181` — these change how every scanner sends requests and have nothing to do with AUD-65 or AUD-66. They are also risky. `pace_wire_send` holds the shared `PolitenessEngine._lock` while it sleeps and while it calls `conn.request(...)`. For plain HTTP, urllib3 opens the TCP connection inside that call. So one slow or hanging connect blocks every scanner thread until the watchdog deadline fires. On top of that, `pace_request` already spaces requests under the same lock, so this adds a second layer of pacing. → Revert these three files to the base commit (`git diff d9ef5ea -- core/politeness.py core/http_client.py test_b19_scanner_robustness.py` should be empty). If the full suite only passes with them (a flaky B19 timing test), say so under a "Deviations" heading in the handoff, with the failing test name and its output. That change belongs in its own batch, and it must not sleep or open connections while holding a lock shared by all threads.
