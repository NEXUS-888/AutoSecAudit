## Notes
- `ScanHttpClient` counters are never reset in `run_plugins`, unlike the engine counters. A reused `Engine` would carry request totals into the next scan's `coverage`. Consider resetting them at scan start.
- `reports/generator.py` adds a `generate()` alias and summary defaults only for the tests. These are out of scope. Prefer calling `generate_html` in the test and reverting them.
- The 100-thread test calls the `increment_*` methods directly rather than going through `request()`. That is acceptable, but one concurrent `request()` test against a fixture would make the claim much stronger.
