Either drop `/graphql/v1` or give discovered paths priority. A no-GraphQL target with no discovered endpoints must still send ≤ 6 requests. (Checked next round by: a fixture serving GraphQL only at `/custom/graphql`, with the scanner given that discovered endpoint, must produce the introspection finding.)
2. [AUD-75] `plugins/graphql_plugin.py:run` Phase 4 / `_check_status_differential`. Per argument, the scanner sends 3 SQLi payloads, then `'` again, `''`, `safe123`, then `'` a third time (`probe_single`), then 3 XSS payloads. That is 7 distinct payloads and up to 9 payload-bearing requests, breaking the "6 payloads per argument" safety cap. → Fix: total payloads per argument ≤ 6. For example, reuse the `'` response from the error-signature loop inside the differential and remove the `probe_single` re-send. Baseline and control values may be excluded from the count. (Checked next round by: a test that counts fixture-logged requests per `field.arg`, excluding baseline/control values, and asserts ≤ 6. A second test with a schema of 25 String args must show at most 20 distinct args probed. Neither cap is asserted today.)
