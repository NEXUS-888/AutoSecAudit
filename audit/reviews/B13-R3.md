## Notes
- `plugins/nmap_plugin.py:70,108`: `protocol = port.get("protocol")` can be `None`. Only `method` is guarded, so `endpoint` and `evidence` would read `80/None`. The fingerprint is still unique per port, so this doesn't block. Applying `protocol = port.get("protocol") or "tcp"` at line 70 would fix both.
- Nikto `param` is cut to 50 characters (`nikto_plugin.py:110,116`). Two different messages on the same URI that share their first 50 characters would collide. That is unlikely with real Nikto output, but a longer cut-off or a short hash of the full message would remove the risk.
