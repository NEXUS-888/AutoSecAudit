## Notes
- The `\b(?:window\.)?open\s*\(` alternative in `_ON_NAV_PATTERN` also matches `xhr.open('GET', '/api')`, which adds a bogus relative link `GET`. This is harmless (same-origin GET) but noisy. Consider anchoring it to `(?:^|[^.\w])(?:window\.)?open\(`.
- `_HEX_ID_SEGMENT_RE` treats words made only of hex letters, 6 or more long, as ids (e.g. `/facade`, `/decade`), and also short hex+digit segments like `e2e` or `b2b`. These pages only get grouped under the cap of 5, so it's non-blocking.
- `test_b22_form_injection.py` gained extra `patch.object(...)` isolation lines that the handoff doesn't mention. No assertions changed, so it isn't a weakening, but the fixer should list it in the handoff.
