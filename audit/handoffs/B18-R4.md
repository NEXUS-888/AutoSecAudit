## Test Suite Stability & Politeness Pacing Fix
- Changes: `core/politeness.py`:
  - **Thread-Safe Pacing Serialization (`pace_request`):** Serialized request spacing wait under `_lock` (`wait_time = self._next_allowed_at - now; if wait_time > 0: time.sleep(wait_time); self._next_allowed_at = time.monotonic() + interval`). Previously, threads computed a future scheduled time, released the lock, and slept in parallel; OS thread-scheduler preemption on Windows caused worker threads to occasionally wake up within <70ms of each other. Serializing the sleep guarantees that every request is strictly spaced by at least `request_interval_ms`.
  - Fixes the intermittent timing failures in `test_b09_shared_http_client.py` (`test_aud25_concurrent_requests_global_rate_limiting` and `test_aud25_engine_http_client_concurrent_requests_paced_by_default_interval`).
