How It Works

The 4-Stage Security Pipeline

From recursive crawling to live NIST CVE enrichment and delta tracking.

Stage 01

Surface Discovery

BFS crawler traverses DOM links and form targets while OpenAPI 3.0 parser automatically seeds all REST endpoints and schemas.

Stage 02

14 Concurrent Plugins

Parallel ThreadPool ($k=8$) launches Nmap, Nikto, SQLi, XSS, SSRF, JWT, and API Abuse scanners concurrently in under ~65 seconds.

Stage 03

Intelligence Layer

Compound-key deduplication merges overlapping alerts (-38.2% noise), enriched with live NIST NVD CVSS scores and OWASP Top 10 tags.

Stage 04

CI/CD Delta Diffing

Computes mathematical diffs ($\Delta_{\text{NEW}}, \Delta_{\text{FIXED}}$) and enforces automated build-breaking exit codes.
